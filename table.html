Name Description Examples

Carbohydrates

 Carbohydrates are the body’s main source of energy. They fuel your muscles, brain, and daily activity. Bread, rice, pasta, cereal, potatoes, corn

Proteins

 Proteins help build and repair body tissues, including muscles, organs, skin, and hair. Eggs, chicken, beans, fish, tofu, nuts

Fats

 Fats provide long-lasting energy and help your brain and body absorb vitamins. Avocados, olive oil, cheese, nuts, salmon

Vitamins

 Vitamins keep your immune system strong and help your body function properly. Fruits, vegetables, milk, carrots, oranges

Minerals

 Minerals help build strong bones, teeth, and maintain healthy blood and muscles. Milk, spinach, bananas, fish, nuts
