FAQ

What foods are rich in omega fatty acids? +
Fatty fish like salmon, sardines, and plant foods like chia seeds, walnuts, and flaxseeds.
What is considered unhealthy food? +
Foods high in sugar, salt, unhealthy fats, and low in nutrients. Like soda, chips, and fast food.
Why should a diet be balanced? +
It gives your body all the nutrients it needs to grow, get energy, and stay healthy.
Difference between healthy and unhealthy food? +
Healthy foods have nutrients. Unhealthy foods have lots of calories but not many nutrients.
