FAQ

  • What foods are rich is omega fatty acids?

    +

    Foods rich in omega fatty acids include fatty fish like salmon, mackerel, sardines, and herring (high in EPA and DHA), and plant-based options like flaxseeds, chia seeds, walnuts, and soybeans (rich in ALA). Other sources include some fortified foods, canola and soybean oils, and certain vegetables like spinach and Brussels sprouts.

  • What is considered as unhealthy food?

    +

    Unhealthy food, often called junk food or ultra-processed food, is defined as food that is low in nutrients like fiber, vitamins, and minerals, but high in calories, sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats. Examples include sugary drinks, fast food, processed meats, and snacks like chips and cookies. Eating too much unhealthy food can lead to health complications, so it should be consumed in moderation.

  • Why should a diet be balanced?

    +

    A diet must be balanced to provide the body with all essential nutrients, including carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, and minerals, which are crucial for energy, growth, and bodily functions.

  • What is the difference between healthy and unhealthy foods?

    +

    Healthy foods are typically nutrient-dense, providing essential vitamins, minerals, and fiber for a low calorie count, while unhealthy foods are often high in calories, unhealthy fats, sugar, and sodium, but low in nutrients. The main difference lies in their nutritional value: healthy foods fuel the body with necessary nutrients, while unhealthy (or "junk") foods are high in "empty calories" with little nutritional benefit.
